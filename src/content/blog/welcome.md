## About This Site

AI Daily News is an automated news aggregator powered by:

- **RSS feeds** from curated sources across AI, software engineering, and science
- **GPT-4o-mini** for intelligent article summarization
- **Astro** for blazing-fast static site generation
- **GitHub Actions** for fully unattended daily publishing

New briefings are published automatically every day at 07:00 UTC. Check back tomorrow for your first AI-curated report!
